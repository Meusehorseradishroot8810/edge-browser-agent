## 🎯 What Is This?

edge-browser-agent lets your Android phone run real web browsers on your Windows computer — completely offline. It uses small AI models that live right on your phone (no cloud, no internet needed) to see what's on your screen, understand it, and click, type, and scroll just like you would.

Think of it as a remote control for your browser, powered by artificial intelligence that fits in your pocket.

The latest version connects your phone's AI to two browsers at once — Google Chrome and Firefox — and lets them talk to each other. In testing, it worked perfectly 10 times out of 10 on a Galaxy S21 phone.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🧠 On-Device AI** — The intelligence runs on your phone using models like Ministral 3 3B, Qwen2.5, and Qwen3. No internet connection required after setup.
- **🖥️ Real Browser Control** — Your phone's AI operates actual desktop browsers (Chrome, Firefox) on your Windows PC — not a simulation.
- **🔄 Multi-Browser Conversations** — The AI can relay live conversations between two browsers, making them work together.
- **📊 Full Transparency** — Every action is logged in JSONL format. You can see exactly what the AI did, step by step.
- **🔁 Offline Replay** — Watch past sessions play back so you can verify or learn from what happened.
- **📝 Raw HTML Control** — Advanced users can directly manipulate the underlying web page code.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Phone won't connect** | Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi. Try disabling VPN on your phone. |
| **Browser doesn't respond** | Close all browser windows and restart the computer application. |
| **AI seems slow** | Close other apps on your phone. Background tasks slow down the AI model. |
| **Connection drops** | Check that your computer didn't go to sleep. Adjust power settings to keep it awake. |
| **Can't find the app** | Look for "edge-browser-agent" in your Start Menu or on your phone's app drawer. |
